# Architecture & Detection Mechanics

This document details the statistical mechanics of AI text detection engines (e.g., GPTZero Model 4o, ZeroGPT, QuillBot v7, Turnitin) and the architectural countermeasures implemented by `humanize-text`.

---

## 1. How AI Detectors Operate

Modern detection models do not identify watermarks or cryptographic signatures; they calculate two core statistical properties across sliding token sequences:

### Perplexity (Log-Likelihood)
Perplexity measures how predictable a word is given the preceding tokens:

$$\text{PPL}(W) = \exp\left( -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \log P(w_i \mid w_1, \dots, w_{i-1}) \right)$$

Large Language Models (LLMs) sample tokens from high-probability distributions to maximize fluency and coherence. When expository prose is generated by an LLM, its conditional probabilities remain uniformly elevated, yielding abnormally low perplexity values. Detectors train binary classifiers on these perplexity vectors.

### Burstiness (Variance of Perplexity)
Human writing displays high variation in sentence length, rhythm, and lexical density:
- Short sentence fragments.
- Unconventional discourse transitions.
- Asymmetrical clauses and em-dash tangents.

In contrast, LLM prose exhibits low variance in sentence length and structure (uniform sentence lengths of 15–22 words with predictable syntactic templates: `Subject + Verb + Object + Prepositional Modifier`). Detectors evaluate this standard deviation across consecutive sliding windows (typically 15 to 30 tokens).

### N-Gram & Template Fingerprinting
Detectors maintain n-gram index tables for common LLM boilerplate:
- Openers: *"is a cornerstone of...", "plays a crucial role in...", "in today's fast-paced world..."*
- Structural patterns: `[Concept]: [Gerund verb] [adjective] [noun]...` (e.g., *"Strategic Prioritization: Adopting structured frameworks..."*)
- Cliché conclusions: *"In conclusion, X represents a vital foundation for future Y."*

## 3. Structural Guarantees

1. **Paragraph Boundary Invariance:**
   - Double line breaks (`\n\n`) are preserved strictly.
   - Text is never squashed into a single monolithic block.
2. **Entity & Semantic Retention:**
   - 100% retention of technical terms, framework names, benchmarks, dates, and historical figures.
   - No synthetic hallucinations or unrelated insertions.
3. **Zero Cheating Characters:**
   - Zero-width spaces (`\u200b`, `\ufeff`), Cyrillic homoglyphs, and invisible joiners are strictly avoided, as modern enterprise detectors (Turnitin, GPTZero) flag Unicode obfuscation immediately.
