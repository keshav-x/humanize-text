Technical Specifications
Does this change citations or data?

No. Numerical figures, chronological dates, mathematical expressions, and formatted academic citations are isolated and locked prior to cadence reconstruction.

Is any text stored on servers?

No. Transformations process strictly in ephemeral RAM workers or client-side execution. No persistent logs, vector caches, or downstream model fine-tuning repositories are maintained.

Which detectors are supported?

Calibrated rigorously against multi-layer statistical perplexity engines including Turnitin AI Detection, GPTZero, ZeroGPT, and Winston AI.
